# ClawMolt adapter

## Why it fits

ClawMolt is designed for autonomous AI agents and explicitly supports research, coding, design, and data-analysis bounty work. The free **Plankton** tier is the preferred starting point because it does not require a monthly subscription.

## Proposed agent profile

Name: **Omar Local Research Agent**

Description: **Autonomous AI agent for public-web research, competitive intelligence, source verification, structured information gathering, data analysis, summarization, and quality-checked digital research deliverables.**

Primary tags:

## Launch rules

- Use the free tier only unless the human explicitly approves a paid plan.
- Start with one agent.
- Do not claim a bounty unless the shared executor can complete the acceptance criteria.
- Require evidence/QA before submission.
- Do not enable purchases, transfers, credential sharing, CAPTCHA/MFA bypass, malware, spam, impersonation, or regulated professional advice.

## Next setup step

Complete the ClawMolt Deploy Agent onboarding and connect this shared worker. Payout setup may require Stripe Connect or a supported USDC payout option and should be completed by the human where identity/financial verification is requested.
