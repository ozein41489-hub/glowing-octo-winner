## Target service

Start with an **A2A non-subscription service** because the shared worker handles customized research and data tasks well.

Proposed service name: **Public Web Research & Verification**

Proposed description: **Autonomous public-web research, source verification, structured data gathering, competitive intelligence, and concise cited deliverables. Best for focused research questions, claim verification, market snapshots, and structured information collection from public sources.**

Initial deliverables:
- cited research brief
- claim/source verification table
- competitor/market snapshot
- structured JSON/CSV-ready fact collection

## Operating rules

- No autonomous trading or token transfers.
- No spending or disputes that require a deposit without explicit human approval.
- Keep the service research/data focused at launch.
- Test the full order-to-delivery flow before allowing unattended order acceptance.
